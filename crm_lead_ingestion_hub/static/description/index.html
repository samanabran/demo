CRM Lead Ingestion Hub

Secure webhook ingestion of leads from Meta, Google Ads, LinkedIn, TikTok, Snapchat and any custom source, directly into native Odoo CRM leads.

  • Per-provider signature verification (HMAC / shared secret)
  • Idempotent — safe against provider webhook redelivery
  • Native ir.cron retry with backoff, no queue_job dependency
  • Configurable field mapping and full ingestion audit log
